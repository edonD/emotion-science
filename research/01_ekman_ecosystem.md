# Paul Ekman Ecosystem Research

## Training Products & Pricing

### Package Tiers
- **Face Basics** — $119 USD, 3-month subscription
  - Micro Expressions Training Tool (METT)
  - Subtle Expressions Training Tool (SETT)
  - Introductory-level, self-paced online

- **Face Advanced** — ~$199-249 USD (estimated), 6-month subscription
  - Four micro expressions training tools
  - Deeper practice in detection and recognition

- **Full Access / Certification** — ~$299+ USD, 12-month subscription
  - All seven training tools
  - Extended practice in micro and subtle expressions
  - Full certified training path

### Training Format
- Exclusively online, web-based applications
- Combination of text, photos, videos, auditory commentary
- Self-paced modules
- Subscription-based (auto-renewal emails 30 days before expiry)
- Periodic discount codes (e.g., 20% off with "ReadMicros")

### The Feelings Field Guide
- 92-card deck + accompanying guidebook
- Explores 7 universal emotions
- Quiz and reflection activities for emotional intelligence
- Marketed for: self-study, partner inquiry, classrooms, team-building, therapy
- 30-day return policy with 15% restocking fee

### Other Products
- Books: "Emotions Revealed" (multiple editions), academic publications
- Atlas of Emotions (online interactive tool, commissioned by Dalai Lama)
- Emotional Intelligence & Deception Training workshops
- Corporate/organizational workshops

## Website Observations
- Dated design aesthetic — feels academic/clinical, not modern tech
- Complex navigation, many sub-pages
- Pricing not always transparent (dynamically loaded)
- Heavy on text, light on visual engagement
- WooCommerce-based shop
- Strong brand authority but weak UX

## Reviews & Criticism

### Positive
- Testimonials claim improved ability to read emotions professionally
- Strong brand recognition — Ekman is THE name in micro-expressions
- Academic credibility (though increasingly questioned)
- Used by law enforcement, intelligence agencies (claimed)

### Negative / Skepticism
- METT training showed only 46.3% deception detection accuracy (near coin-flip)
- Critics note Ekman no longer publishes in peer-reviewed journals
- Original 1970s micro-expression work never formally published
- Some view products as overpriced for what's delivered
- "One of a thousand people who will happily take your money" — security professional
- Scientific community increasingly skeptical of deception detection claims

## Key Opportunity
Ekman's brand is strong but his platform is outdated. The science is contested but the market demand is real. A modern, transparent, science-honest platform could capture professionals who want training but are put off by the dated UX and opaque pricing.
