# Competitor Analysis

## Paul Ekman Group (paulekman.com)
- **Pricing**: $119 (Face Basics) to $299+ (Full Access)
- **Strengths**: Brand authority, research legacy, comprehensive FACS system
- **Weaknesses**: Dated website, opaque pricing, questionable scientific claims, no modern UX
- **Market position**: The incumbent — name recognition is everything

## Humintell (humintell.com)
- **Founded by**: Dr. David Matsumoto (Ekman's former student)
- **Focus**: Microexpressions, gesture, nonverbal behavior, culture, emotion
- **Offerings**: Online training courses, corporate programs, threat assessment
- **Strengths**: Strong academic credentials, practical focus
- **Weaknesses**: Low brand awareness compared to Ekman, academic-feeling platform

## Science of People (scienceofpeople.com)
- **Founded by**: Vanessa Van Edwards
- **Focus**: People skills, body language, communication, charisma
- **Key product**: People School — comprehensive virtual coaching program
- **Content**: Body Language Mastery training, books, speaking
- **Strengths**: Modern brand, strong content marketing, relatable presenter, wide appeal
- **Weaknesses**: More pop-psychology than hard science, broad focus dilutes expertise

## Other Players
- **Center for Body Language** — European-focused, certification programs
- **Joe Navarro** — Former FBI, books on body language, skeptical of deception detection
- **MasterClass** (Vanessa Van Edwards course) — Premium production, general audience
- **CreativeLive** — Body language courses, creative professional audience
- **Global Gurus** — Top 30 Body Language Professionals rankings

## Market Gaps We Can Exploit
1. **No one owns "modern + scientific"** — Ekman is old-school, Science of People is pop
2. **Pricing transparency** — Most competitors hide or obfuscate pricing
3. **Honest science** — Nobody clearly states what training CAN and CANNOT do
4. **UX/Design quality** — Every competitor has a mediocre-to-poor website
5. **Professional positioning** — Gap between "self-help body language" and "academic FACS training"
6. **Tech-forward aesthetic** — ASCII art, data visualization, modern stack = unique identity
