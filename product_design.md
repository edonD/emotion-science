# Product Design — EmotionLens

## Brand Identity

### Name: **EmotionLens**
A modern behavioral intelligence platform. Not a self-help product — a professional tool for people who read people.

### Brand Voice
- Scientific precision, not pop psychology
- Confident authority, not academic stuffiness
- Practical application, not theoretical abstraction
- Honest about what the science supports (and what it doesn't)

### Visual Identity
- Clean, technical aesthetic with ASCII art signature elements
- Warm off-white backgrounds with deep charcoal text
- Teal accent color for CTAs and highlights
- Monospace typography for data and technical content
- Sans-serif for body copy

### Key Differentiators from Ekman
| Ekman | EmotionLens |
|-------|-------------|
| Dated website, poor UX | Modern, minimalist, premium design |
| Opaque pricing | Transparent pricing, clear tiers |
| Claims "lie detection" | Honest: recognition ≠ deception detection |
| Academic/clinical feel | Professional/tech-forward feel |
| Subscription-based access | One-time purchase with lifetime updates |
| Text-heavy learning | Visual, interactive, data-driven learning |

## Three Product Tiers

### Tier 1: Foundations — $89

**Tagline**: "See what others miss."

**Who it's for**: Professionals who want to understand the science of emotion and begin recognizing micro-expressions in everyday interactions.

**What's included**:
- **Module 1**: The Science of Emotion — evolutionary origins, cross-cultural research, the 7 universal emotions with deep dives into each
- **Module 2**: Micro-Expression Recognition — what they are, how they form, duration (40-67ms), involuntary nature
- **Module 3**: The 7 Expressions in Practice — photo and video-based recognition training for anger, contempt, disgust, fear, happiness, sadness, surprise
- **Module 4**: Emotional Intelligence Foundations — self-awareness, empathy, reading emotional context
- **Interactive quizzes** — test your recognition speed and accuracy after each module
- **Progress tracking** — see your recognition accuracy improve over time
- **Self-paced** — complete in 8-12 hours, access for 12 months

**Pricing rationale**: Undercuts Ekman's Face Basics ($119) while offering more structured curriculum and better UX. Accessible entry point for curious professionals.

---

### Tier 2: Professional — $229

**Tagline**: "The complete system for reading people."

**Who it's for**: Therapists, negotiators, HR professionals, investigators, and leaders who need systematic emotion reading skills for their work.

**What's included**:
- Everything in Foundations, plus:
- **Module 5**: FACS Essentials — Action Units, facial muscle anatomy, systematic coding
- **Module 6**: Advanced Detection — subtle expressions, partial expressions, masked emotions, micro-expression speed training
- **Module 7**: Contextual Reading — integrating facial expressions with voice, body language, and situational context
- **Module 8**: Professional Applications — negotiation scenarios, interview analysis, therapeutic observation, team dynamics
- **Module 9**: The Limits of Reading — what science says about deception detection, cognitive biases, avoiding overconfidence
- **Practice library** — 500+ expression samples with FACS annotations
- **Timed exercises** — train your recognition speed from 500ms down to 100ms
- **Certificate of completion** — verifiable digital certificate
- **Self-paced** — complete in 25-35 hours, lifetime access

**Pricing rationale**: Competitive with Ekman's mid-tier (~$199-249) but includes more practical application content and honest scientific framing. The "most popular" tier.

---

### Tier 3: Expert Mastery — $399

**Tagline**: "Master the science. Lead the field."

**Who it's for**: Researchers, senior practitioners, FACS certification candidates, and professionals who want the deepest possible training in emotion science.

**What's included**:
- Everything in Foundations + Professional, plus:
- **Module 10**: Complete FACS Training — all 44+ Action Units, intensity coding (A-E), modifiers, combination analysis
- **Module 11**: Emotional Profiling — building behavioral baselines, tracking emotional patterns over time, individual variation
- **Module 12**: Advanced Contextual Analysis — multi-channel integration, cultural considerations, power dynamics
- **Module 13**: Case Studies — real-world analysis of public figures, negotiation footage, therapy sessions (anonymized)
- **Module 14**: Speed Mastery — timed micro-expression detection exercises pushing below 40ms recognition threshold
- **Module 15**: The Research Frontier — current papers, AI vs human recognition, where the science is heading
- **FACS certification preparation** — structured path to certification readiness
- **Private community** — peer network of trained professionals
- **Ongoing content updates** — new modules, research summaries, case studies
- **1-on-1 feedback session** — 30-minute expert review of your progress
- **Self-paced** — complete in 60-80 hours, lifetime access + updates

**Pricing rationale**: Below Ekman's top tier (~$299+ for tools alone) while offering dramatically more depth. The "lifetime access + updates" model justifies the premium.

## Positioning Statement

EmotionLens is the modern platform for evidence-based emotion recognition training. Built on decades of FACS research, refined by the latest science, and designed for professionals who need to read people accurately — not a magic lie detector, but a systematic skill backed by real data.

We acknowledge the scientific legacy while being transparent about its limits. That honesty is our competitive advantage.
